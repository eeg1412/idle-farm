## 绘制提示

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one brand-new raster technology badge for a cozy Japanese pixel-art farming game. Classic SNES inventory sprite, clearly hand-pixelled around native 40x40 px with stepped warm-dark outline, clusters of deliberate midtone pixels and a small palette. Strong silhouette readable at 22–32 px against warm ivory. Center the full badge with safe margins on a square genuine transparent RGBA background. No text, lettering, extra symbols, backdrop, checkerboard, drop shadow, glow, soft antialias or vector edges.

- Draw a compact copper spyglass angled upward by a small amount over a single folded ivory market sheet with a teal edge; the spyglass is the main shape. Glass has one muted aquamarine highlight, parchment has no words or numbers. Market forecast technology.
- Draw a chunky round iron-and-brass market control dial with a single cyan enamel pointer and three short engraved notches in the copper rim. Strong wide silhouette; readable as a hand-adjusted dial or knob. No arrows, gear teeth or text. Market reset technology.
